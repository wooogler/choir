CHOIR

A Slack assistant that helps teams answer questions from GitHub-backed documentation and turn useful conversations into durable organizational knowledge.

System

Knowledge workflows where the conversation already happens

CHOIR connects Slack, GitHub markdown repositories, and language models into a lightweight loop for organizational knowledge management.

01

Question Answering

Members can ask CHOIR questions in Slack and receive answers grounded in the team's documentation.

02

Document Update Suggestions

When new knowledge appears in a conversation, CHOIR drafts targeted updates for review.

03

GitHub Sync

Approved changes are applied to markdown files, keeping the source of truth in version control.

Research

Designed as a research prototype for collaborative knowledge maintenance

CHOIR explores how Slack-based assistants can reduce the distance between informal workplace knowledge and maintained documentation. Add the paper link, venue, authors, and BibTeX here when they are ready.
